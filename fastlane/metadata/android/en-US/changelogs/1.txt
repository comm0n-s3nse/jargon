First release of Jargon, a fork of Kaiboard.
• Text appears after you finish dictating, as one piece, with correct spacing and capitalization
• Hold the microphone to talk; drag up to cancel; the key shows the microphone level
• Spoken punctuation and emoji: "question mark", "smiley face", "facepalm"…
• Hold backspace to delete word by word; drag it left to undo
• Flick enter up to switch keyboard; hold it for settings
• Three engines: Whisper, Parakeet (Fluid Speech Mini) and Canary 1B, which translates between 25 languages
• Models download in the app, checksum-verified, after you confirm
• Times itself on your phone to pick the fastest thread count
