A voice typing keyboard that recognizes your speech <b>on your phone</b>: what you say is never uploaded. The only thing it uses the internet for is downloading a speech model, when you tap Download. It is a fork of Kaiboard by Kai, built on whisper.cpp.

It is a dictation strip, not a full keyboard: keep your normal keyboard for typing and switch to this one when you want to talk. It comes with the Whisper tiny.en model for English.

<b>Dictating</b>
• Tap the microphone to start, tap the check mark to finish. Or hold the microphone while you speak and let go to finish; drag up while holding to throw the recording away.
• Text appears only when you finish, as one piece, with the spacing and capitalization the sentence around the cursor needs.
• Highlight text and dictate to replace it.
• Silence at the start and end of a recording is trimmed, so Whisper is not handed silence to invent words from.

<b>Editing</b>
• Backspace deletes a character, or holds to repeat and then delete a word at a time.
• Drag backspace to the left to undo, even a long run of deleted text.
• Enter sends, searches or adds a new line, whichever the field expects. Pull it up to open settings.

<b>Models and languages</b>
• Download a recommended model from the settings screen, or add your own: other Whisper models (the multilingual ones understand many languages, and can translate) or Parakeet models in .gguf format. Only one is kept in memory at a time.
• The keyboard times itself once on your phone and picks the fastest thread count, which is often fewer than all of your cores.
